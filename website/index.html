Features

Everything you'd want from a messenger — and a few things you wouldn't expect

Chatterbox bundles real-time chat, calling, and a lightweight social feed into one app, built on Firebase for reliable sync across your devices.

Rich real-time messaging

Text, photos, video, voice notes, GIFs, and file sharing with read receipts, reactions, and message translation — synced instantly via Cloud Firestore.

Voice & video calls

Peer-to-peer voice and video calling built on WebRTC, right inside your chats — no third-party app required.

Smart chat tools

AI-assisted chat summaries, smart replies, voice transcription, and message translation to keep up with busy conversations.

Fun extras

Chat Wrapped (your year in review), Chat Rituals, a shared Quote Wall, collaborative playlists, a shared whiteboard, countdowns, and scheduled messages.

Built to stay in sync

Push notifications, offline caching, and background sync mean your chats are up to date the moment you open the app.

Privacy & security

Your conversations, locked down

Chatterbox treats privacy as a feature, not an afterthought.

App Lock

Biometric or PIN lock with configurable auto-lock timing.

Secure Vault

A protected space for your most sensitive photos, files, and notes.

View-once media

Photos and videos that disappear after they're viewed.

Message expiry

Set chats to auto-delete messages from 1 hour up to 30 days.

Remote wipe

Clear local data remotely if a device is lost or stolen.

Dead man's switch

Automatically wipe local data after a period of inactivity you choose.

Blocking & visibility

Block anyone. Nobody can find you by searching — an invite link you hand out is the only way in, and it works once.

Single-session security

Signing in on a new device automatically signs out the old one.
